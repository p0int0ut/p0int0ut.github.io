Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

Thank you for playing our games. Aimlock ("we," "our," or "us") develops and publishes games and related services. This Privacy Policy applies to our games, websites, and related support services (collectively, the "Services"). It explains what information may be collected when you use the Services, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to provide your name, postal address, or phone number to play our games. The categories of information processed depend on the Service you use and may include:

  • Gameplay and usage data: game sessions, progress, level events, interactions, and similar gameplay statistics.
  • Device and technical data: device type, operating system and version, app version, language, country or approximate region, and technical identifiers generated by a Service or analytics provider.
  • Diagnostics: crash, error, and performance information.
  • Support communications: your email address and any information you choose to include if you contact us.

Our analytics provider may receive an IP address when data is transmitted and may use it to derive an approximate location. We do not use this information to identify your precise location.

2. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • operate and maintain the Services;
  • understand how players use our games;
  • improve gameplay, difficulty, stability, and performance;
  • detect and fix errors or crashes; and
  • respond when you contact us for support.

3. Analytics service

We use GameAnalytics to understand gameplay and improve our games. GameAnalytics processes analytics data on our behalf. Depending on the game and platform, its processing may include gameplay events, device and platform information, technical identifiers, and approximate location information.

For more information, review the GameAnalytics Developer Policy and GameAnalytics Trust Center.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers such as GameAnalytics only as needed to operate, analyze, support, secure, or improve the Services. We may also disclose information when required by law, necessary to protect legal rights, or necessary to prevent fraud or security threats.

5. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including operating and improving the Services, responding to support requests, and meeting legal obligations. Analytics information is also subject to GameAnalytics' retention practices.

6. Data security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also uninstall a game to stop future collection from that installation.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the email address below. We may need information reasonably necessary to verify and fulfill your request.

8. Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a Service, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers of personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice before a material change takes effect.
